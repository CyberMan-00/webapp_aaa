Amazon Prime: Why You should have it?

Amazon is the world’s largest online retailer and one of the biggest companies globally. As of February 2024, Amazon's market capitalization is approximately $1.82 trillion, placing it among the top five companies worldwide.

For customers, this comes with two major benefits. First, Amazon’s size allows it to offer low prices and minimal fees. Second, the company constantly attracts new shoppers with discounts and a range of services. One of the standout offerings is Amazon Prime, a membership program launched in 2005. Amazon actively promotes it, and subscribers get significant perks.

Amazon Prime is more than just a subscription for fast shipping—it offers a range of perks and services that make it a valuable tool for many shoppers just for 49 PLN per year in Poland. The first 30 days are free. Here’s everything you need to know to decide if Amazon Prime is worth it for you.

What is Amazon Prime?

Amazon Prime is a paid membership that provides subscribers with numerous perks. It’s most famous for offering free one- or two-day shipping on millions of items sold on Amazon. As of the most recent update, there are more than 200 million Prime members worldwide. However, beyond shipping benefits, Amazon Prime includes a suite of entertainment options, exclusive deals, and discounts, making it a multipurpose subscription for its users.

Key Benefits of Amazon Prime

  • Free and Fast Shipping: Enjoy free delivery on all Prime-marked products, with no minimum order value. You can even buy items as low as 5 PLN and still get them shipped for free.
  • Prime Video: Amazon’s streaming service rivals Netflix, offering a wide variety of TV shows and movies, from classics to modern hits.
  • Prime Gaming: previously known as Twitch Prime, offers members free in-game content, exclusive loot, and monthly free PC games that members can keep. It also includes a free Twitch channel subscription every month.
  • Amazon Prime Early Access: Members get 30-minute early access to Lightning Deals and exclusive promotions throughout the year.
  • Amazon Prime Day: This annual sale event is exclusive to Prime subscribers. It’s similar to "Black Friday" but takes place in July, offering massive discounts across various categories.

How to Get Started with Amazon Prime:

  1. Visit the Prime page
  2. Click on "Start Your 30-Day Free Trial."
  3. After the trial period, it’s recommended to choose the yearly subscription, which costs just 49 PLN.
  4. Start shopping and enjoy all the perks!
